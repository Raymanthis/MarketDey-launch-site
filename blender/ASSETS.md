# Third-party assets and tools (Blender look development)

Only CC0 assets are used in the rendered street. Everything else in `blender/lookdev/` (buildings,
street, poles, wires, trees, keke napep, danfo, okada, cars, wrappers, head-ties and head basins)
is modelled procedurally by our own scripts.

## Used

| Asset | Where | Source | Licence | Author | In git? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MakeHuman system assets: base mesh, skins, clothes (`male_casualsuit02..06`, `male_worksuit01`, `female_casualsuit01`, `female_elegantsuit01`), shoes, hair (`short01/02/04`) | downloaded to `blender/assets/vendor/makehuman-system-assets-cc0/` (281 MB zip), unpacked into the MPFB user-data folder | http://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip | CC0 1.0 (every entry in `packs/makehuman_system_assets.json` is tagged `"license": "CC0"`) | MakeHuman team ("makehuman_system") | No — gitignored, too large |
| MPFB 2.0.18 (MakeHuman plugin for Blender) — *tool*, used to build and pose the people | `blender/assets/vendor/mpfb-extension/` (zip and an isolated `BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES` folder) | https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/ | GPL-3.0-or-later (code only; its outputs are not covered) | Joel Palmius / MakeHuman community | No — gitignored |
| `blender/assets/people/people.blend` — nine posed, clothed pedestrians baked to plain meshes | generated by `blender/lookdev/make_people.py` | derived from the MakeHuman CC0 assets above | CC0-derived | ours | Yes (7.3 MB) |

The MakeHuman base mesh and targets ship inside MPFB under CC0 as well (MakeHuman's licence makes
the mesh and any exported character CC0).

### Rebuilding `people.blend`

```sh
V=blender/assets/vendor
mkdir -p $V/makehuman-system-assets-cc0 $V/mpfb-extension
curl -L -o $V/makehuman-system-assets-cc0/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip \
  http://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip
curl -L -o $V/mpfb-extension/mpfb-v2.0.18.zip \
  'https://extensions.blender.org/download/sha256:c075b3e9b94b8e5556a5f03f4653602d02ea306de08126e8f55ed6dee28a2dad/add-on-mpfb-v2.0.18.zip'
export BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES="$PWD/$V/mpfb-extension/blender-user"   # keeps your own Blender config untouched
blender -b --factory-startup --command extension install-file -r user_default -e $V/mpfb-extension/mpfb-v2.0.18.zip
unzip -q $V/makehuman-system-assets-cc0/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip \
  -d "$BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES/extensions/.user/user_default/mpfb/data"
blender -b --factory-startup -P blender/lookdev/make_people.py -- --sheet
```

## Evaluated and rejected

| Asset | Why not |
|---|---|
| Quaternius *Posed Background Characters* and *Realistic Car Pack* (quaternius.com) | The pack pages still say CC0, but quaternius.com/license.html (updated 2026-08-28) now puts every asset under the *Quaternius Asset License* (no redistribution, "regardless of the specific site"), and that licence governs assets obtained now. Not CC0, so the partial downloads were deleted. The cars were also faceted low-poly, which Freestyle inks facet by facet. |
| Kenney *Car Kit* (kenney.nl, CC0) | CC0, but toy proportions (chunky, oversized wheels). Deleted after evaluation. |
| Poly Haven models (CC0) | No people or usable cars (only `covered_car`). Scanned props ink as noisy contours. Not needed. |
| Sapling Tree Gen | Not bundled with Blender 5.2 (it moved to the extensions platform). Trees are generated by our own script, `blender/lookdev/trees.py`. |
